Welcome

Welcome to Southmere Academy

On behalf of everyone at Southmere Primary Academy, welcome to our website. Our aim is to give you information and a taste of life at our Academy. We are always keen to welcome visitors, so please contact us if you would like to arrange a visit or require more information.

The academy has a strong ethos with highly qualified and dedicated staff. We believe in supporting the development of successful, well-rounded children who will become positive members of the community, and we have high expectations for all of our children in terms of progress and attainment.
