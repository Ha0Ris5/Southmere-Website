Key Information

Everything parents and carers need to know

A quick reference to the practical information you're most likely to need. If you can't find what you're looking for, please get in touch and we'll be happy to help.

Academy Day

Gates open at 8:45am, with registration at 9:00am. The school day finishes at 3:15pm for all year groups.

Attendance

We expect all pupils to attend regularly and on time. Please call the office before 9:00am if your child is unwell.

Uniform

Navy jumper or cardigan, white polo shirt, grey or navy trousers/skirt, and black school shoes.

Catering

Hot school lunches are prepared on-site daily. Menus are shared with parents each term.

Term Dates

Term dates for the current academic year are published on our calendar, including inset days.

Safeguarding

The safety and wellbeing of every child is our highest priority. Contact us if you have a concern.
