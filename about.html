About Us

Welcome to Southmere Academy

On behalf of everyone at Southmere Primary Academy, welcome to our website. Our aim is to give you information and a taste of life at our Academy. We are always keen to welcome visitors to our Academy, so please contact us if you would like to arrange a visit or require more information.

On the 1st January 2019, Southmere Primary Academy joined Delta Academies Trust.

The academy has a strong ethos with highly qualified and dedicated staff. We believe in supporting the development of successful, well-rounded children who will become positive members of the community. We have high expectations for all of our children in terms of progress and attainment.

Our aim is to provide high standards of care, teaching, learning and leadership so that all our children achieve their academic potential whilst learning to become confident and resilient children.

Southmere has a 60 intake on entry from Reception to Year 6, with a 15 place morning-only Nursery provision. From September 2025, Southmere's pupil admission numbers will reduce from 60 places to 45 places in Reception.

Community relationships are important to us, and we work hard to create partnerships between school staff and families.

Our Academy

Southmere Primary Academy is a great place to learn and work. Our school motto is ‘Be the Best You Can Be’, and we believe that all children, irrespective of their background, can succeed and achieve their ambitions with our core values of Be Safe, Be Kind, Be Ready and Be Brave.

It is our mission to offer every child a high quality education in a supportive, safe and challenging environment in which they feel valued and are inspired to become life-long learners who aim to ‘be the best they can be’.

Mrs R Binns, Principal

Delta Academies Trust

A Trust that supports every academy

Southmere Primary Academy is proud to be part of Delta Academies Trust, joining on 1st January 2019. Being part of the Trust means access to shared expertise, resources and opportunities across a family of academies, all working towards the same mission: providing all children with the best possible education to enable them to reach their full potential.
